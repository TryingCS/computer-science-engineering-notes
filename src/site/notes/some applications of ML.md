#ml 
Healthcare and bioinformatics 💊
➢ Anomaly detection in medical signals (ECG, EEG).
➢ Prediction of disease presence from clinical variables.
➢ Genetic data analysis.
🔴Algorithms: SVM, Naïve Bayes, logistic
regression.
***
Finance and economics 💰️
➢ Fraud detection in banking transactions.
➢ Credit risk assessment (customer score).
➢ Time series forecasting (interest rates, stock prices)..
🔴Algorithms: regressions, Random Forest...
***
Industry and engineering🏭️
➢ Predictive maintenance (anticipating failures using
sensors).
➢ Quality control (anomaly detection in measurements).
➢ Optimization of industrial processes.
🔴Algorithms: SVM, decision trees ..
***
Web and e-commerce🕸️
➢ Similarity-based recommendations (collaborative
filtering, k-NN).
➢ Targeted advertising.
➢ Ranking of search results.
🔴Algorithms: k-NN, matrix factorization, gradient
boostingApplications of Machine Learning
Transportation and logistics✈️
➢ Route optimization.
➢ Intelligent traffic management.
➢ Demand prediction for transport or delivery..
🔴Typical algorithms : regression, trees, reinforcement
learning.
***
Data analytics and business intelligence📊
➢ Customer segmentation (clustering).
in a supermarket, distinguishing customers who frequently buy
organic products from those who prefer promotions, etc.
➢ Revenue or sales prediction (regression).
predicting monthly sales based on advertising budget, season, or
past trends.
➢ Detection of churn (of churn).
 telecom operator seeks to predict which subscribers will cancel
their contract
🔴algorithms: K-means, logistic regression, decision
trees.
***
Other emerging sectors (with classical methods)🔀
➢ Energy⚡️: consumption forecasting, optimization of electrical
grids.
➢ Agriculture🌱 : soil and crop classification from sensor data.
➢ Security🔐: network intrusion detection, system anomalies.
