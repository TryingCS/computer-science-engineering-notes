#BI
### 1. Why "Business Intelligence"?
*   **Business:** The *domain* you are solving for (tracking sales, optimizing inventory, measuring KPIs).
*   **Intelligence:** The *output*.
* It is not just storing data; it is transforming raw, messy data into actionable insights and knowledge.

### 2. Why is it a CS/AI Course?
It is heavily technical. You are not just learning business theory; you are building the systems that make business analysis possible. The CS components include:
*   **Data Engineering:** Building automated ETL pipelines, writing scripts for Web Scraping, integrating APIs, and implementing OCR.
*   **Database Architecture:** Designing complex multidimensional models (Star/Snowflake schemas), managing data granularity, and optimizing storage through normalization.
*   **Advanced Programming:** Writing complex analytical SQL (Window Functions, CTEs, subqueries) and using Python/Pandas/Scikit-Learn for data cleaning (`fillna`, `pd.to_datetime`, `SimpleImputer`).
*   **System Performance:** Deciding between Batch vs. Stream loading, Serial vs. Parallel processing, and Push vs. Pull methods to handle millions of records efficiently.

### 3. The AI Connection
ML models suffer from "Garbage In, Garbage Out." BI teaches you how to build the clean, structured, historical Data Warehouse that reliable AI and predictive analytics depend on.
